About Me!

Hello, I'm Snickerdoodle Demon (I totally don't have any other name) and I'm a graduate with a degree in Creative Writing... And yet I used to work as an Admin for an IT company... And then I was made redundant. Funny how life works sometimes...

I have various hobbies including writing (shocking, I know), web dev and playing games even though I'm bad at them. I also aspire to make my own games one day too!

I value creativity, freedom and light-hearted fun above most other things in life. I suffer with my mental health so it has always been important to me to create art that reflects the exact opposite of my depression. Life is good sometimes -- and so are people. I want to create projects with themes of friendship and hope -- as well as some badassery along the way. ;)

So I hope you stick around to witness all of my totally cool, 100% awesome-sauce, not-at-all-geeky antics! Whoop whoop!
